Research

Black holes are never alone. I model the matter around them and ask which of its fingerprints survive in the data we actually have: EHT images and X-ray timing.

  1. 01

    Black holes and anisotropic matter fields

    Spacetimes of black holes surrounded by anisotropic fluids: horizons, geodesics and the circular orbits that set what an observer sees.

    Related papers →
  2. 02

    Gravitational lensing and shadows

    Strong lensing in plasma and the size and shape of black hole shadows, compared with EHT observations of M87* and Sgr A*.

    Related papers →
  3. 03

    Quasi-periodic oscillations

    QPO frequencies of X-ray binaries as a probe of strong gravity, with Bayesian MCMC constraints on black hole parameters.

    Related papers →
  4. 04

    Thermodynamics and chaos

    Black hole phase transitions and their connection to Lyapunov exponents of unstable photon orbits.

    Related papers →
  5. 05

    Cosmology and dark matter

    Dark matter halos around rotating black holes and how they imprint on shadows and orbital frequencies.
