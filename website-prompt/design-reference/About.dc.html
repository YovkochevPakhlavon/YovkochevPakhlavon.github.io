About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working on black hole physics. My research asks how matter around a black hole — anisotropic fluids, plasma, dark matter halos — changes what we can observe: the shadow, lensed images and the oscillations of accreting matter.

In November 2026 I defended my PhD thesis, Theoretical and Observational Studies of Black Holes Surrounded by Anisotropic Matter Fields, supervised by Ahmadjon Abdujabbarov. I am an assistant at the Department of Physics, Samarkand State Pedagogical Institute, and I develop the open-source code BlackPy.
