title: Interactive tools
description: Black-hole shadow calculator and QPO explorer — photon sphere and shadow radius against EHT constraints, orbital and epicyclic frequencies against X-ray binary QPOs.
---

Two small calculators built on the same formulas as my research code. Choose a metric and its parameters: the shadow calculator compares the shadow with the EHT measurements of M87* and Sgr A*, and the QPO explorer plots orbital and epicyclic frequencies against the twin-peak QPOs of X-ray binaries.
