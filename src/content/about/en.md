I am a theoretical physicist working on the gravitational and astrophysical aspects of black holes and on cosmology. My research asks how matter around a black hole — anisotropic fluids, plasma, magnetic fields — changes what we can observe: the shadow, lensed images, the motion of particles and the oscillations of accreting matter. I also work on black hole thermodynamics and on numerical methods for fractional differential equations.

In November 2026 I defended my PhD thesis, _Theoretical and Observational Studies of Black Holes Surrounded by Anisotropic Matter Fields_, supervised by Prof. Ahmadjon Abdujabbarov. I am an assistant at the Department of Physics, Samarkand State Pedagogical Institute, and I develop the open-source code BlackPy.

I studied physics (BSc) and mathematical physics (MSc) at the National University of Uzbekistan and worked as a junior researcher at the Ulugh Beg Astronomical Institute. I have spent research visits at the Silesian University in Opava (Erasmus+) and at Zhejiang Normal University. Away from physics I play table tennis and basketball, train for triathlon and take part in rogaining.
